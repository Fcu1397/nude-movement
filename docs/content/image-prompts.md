# AI 圖片生成 Prompts (Image Prompts)

此清單用於在正式照片到位前，生成佔位用 (Placeholder) 的圖片。

> **目前狀態**：`public/images/placeholder/` 內已放入暖色低對比的純色佔位圖（由腳本產生，非 AI 生成），比例與檔名皆已正確，可直接用於版面檢視。若要更接近真實的暫用圖，可用以下 prompt 生成後**覆蓋同名檔案**，不需要改程式。
風格一致性：Japanese Minimal, Boutique Studio, Warm Beige and Ivory tones, Soft Natural Light, Cinematic, Editorial.

---

## 1. Hero 圖片 (4:5)
**用途**：首頁主視覺 (hero.jpg)
**Aspect Ratio**: 4:5

**Prompt**:
> A cinematic and editorial photograph of a dancer's graceful pose in a bright, minimalist boutique movement studio. Warm natural side light casting soft shadows on a light oak wood floor. The dancer is wearing soft nude and ivory studio wear. Focus on the graceful lines of the arms and back, shot from a slight angle. The composition places the subject to one side, with extensive empty space (negative space) and a clean off-white wall on the other. Quiet, serene, mature, quiet luxury aesthetic. No recognizable face.

**Negative Prompt**:
> Face, heavy makeup, high contrast, colorful neon lights, dark shadows, black clothing, gym equipment, text, watermark, cluttered background, stage lighting.

---

## 2. Ballet & Contemporary 課程圖片 (3:4)
**用途**：芭蕾與抒情當代區塊 (class-ballet.jpg)
**Aspect Ratio**: 3:4

**Prompt**:
> A close-up editorial shot of a dancer's legs and feet wearing pale pink ballet slippers on a warm wooden floor in a minimalist studio. Soft natural sunlight filtering into the room. The posture shows control and technique. Ivory and beige color palette, soft and calm atmosphere. Shallow depth of field, elegant and mature aesthetic. No face visible.

**Negative Prompt**:
> Face, full body, children, bright saturated colors, high contrast, text, logo, black clothes, stage, performance.

---

## 3. Gyrokinesis & Stretch 課程圖片 (3:4)
**用途**：墊上嬋柔與拉筋伸展區塊 (class-stretch.jpg)
**Aspect Ratio**: 3:4

**Prompt**:
> An aesthetic, editorial photograph of a person doing a gentle stretching and mobility exercise on a beige yoga mat in a sunlit minimal room. Warm ivory and light beige tones. The focus is on the fluidity of the movement and the release of tension in the back and arms. Shot from behind or a low angle, obscuring the face. Quiet luxury wellness studio vibe. Soft natural light, calming mood.

**Negative Prompt**:
> Face, intense fitness workout, sweat, dumbbells, bright colors, heavy contrast, text, watermark, dark environment.

---

## 4. OG 分享圖片 (1.9:1)
**用途**：社群分享預覽圖 (og.jpg)
**Aspect Ratio**: 16:9 或 1.9:1 (1200x630)

**Prompt**:
> A wide editorial shot of an empty, beautiful Japanese minimal boutique dance studio. Light oak wooden floors, soft ivory walls, and a large window letting in warm, diffused natural morning light. A serene and inviting space. The composition is clean and balanced, keeping the centre uncluttered so social platforms can crop it safely. Quiet luxury, calm, wellness vibe.

**Negative Prompt**:
> People, face, text, logo, cluttered space, gym equipment, high contrast, dark shadows, neon lights.
